About Me

Turning complex stories
into content that connects

I am a content writer and digital storyteller with a broadcasting background. My degree is in BA Broadcasting, but what I actually do — whether it's a scripted broadcast segment or a five-day institutional event — is the same core skill: research a topic quickly, understand who I'm writing for, and turn it into content that's clear, accurate, and worth someone's attention.

Four years of producing large-scale live productions, institutional events, and daily broadcast content means I've already built the habits a content role depends on — tight deadlines, real audiences, zero tolerance for confusing or inaccurate messaging. I've written and produced program content for graduation ceremonies and institutional milestones, coordinated messaging and stakeholder communication for a citywide humanitarian celebration, and led a daily morning broadcast recognized as Best Morning Show — planning segments, writing scripts, and keeping tone and branding consistent episode after episode.

So the offer is simple: a content writer who already knows how to research, translate technical or policy information into accessible stories, and produce across formats — articles, newsletters, social posts, and event coverage — with the added instinct for what keeps an audience engaged from headline to end.
